Stackly started on the plant floor, not in a boardroom. Here's the short version of how we got from a single line in Bengaluru to a system running across three continents.

BENGALURU / LINE 01 / 2019
WHY WE STARTED HERE

The floor always knew first. Nobody upstream was listening.

Origin
Three of us spent a decade fixing the same outage from three different job titles.
Insight
Every factory already generates the signal it needs — it's just scattered across ten systems that don't talk.
Bet
If we made that signal legible in one place, plants could act on it in minutes, not weeks.
HOW WE GOT HERE

Six years, one thesis.

2019
Founded on the plant floor
Started as a side project connecting one welding cell's PLC data to a spreadsheet nobody had to refresh by hand.
2020
First multi-line deployment
Expanded past the pilot line — the same model held across five different machine vendors.
2021
First multi-site rollout
Deployed across three facilities for one automotive supplier, proving the system scales beyond a single plant.
2023
Elastic infrastructure released
Rebuilt the ingestion layer so new sites could connect in days instead of quarters.
2025
24/7 monitoring launched
Added live alerting and an on-call desk so signal turns into action outside business hours too.

HOW WE WORK

Three habits that shape every engagement.

Habit 01
Ship to one line first

We never start with the whole facility. One line, one clear win, then expand.

Habit 02
Sit with the operators

Every deployment starts with a week on the floor, not a kickoff deck.

Habit 03
Instrument, then automate

We earn the right to automate a decision by first proving we can see it clearly.
