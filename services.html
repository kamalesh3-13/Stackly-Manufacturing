THE SIX MODULES

One operating layer, six things it actually does.

Deploy one module or all six — each runs on the same live data model, so nothing gets rebuilt when you add the next.

01
Production Intelligence

Live line status, OEE and throughput in one view, pulled straight from PLCs and MES — no manual logging.

02
Predictive Maintenance

Flags drift before it becomes downtime, using signal from equipment you already have on the floor.

03
Quality & Traceability

Full genealogy from raw material to shipped unit, with scrap and rework caught shifts earlier.

04
Supply Chain Visibility

Connects plant-floor signal to inbound and outbound flow, so shortages show up before they stall a line.

05
Energy Optimization

Benchmarks energy per unit, not per hour, and surfaces where usage is drifting from baseline.

06
Workforce & Scheduling

Matches shift staffing to real demand and routes alerts to whoever's actually on the floor.
